Made for the remote

Five buttons are all you need. Hover a button to see what it does.

  • ◀ ▶Move between your apps
  • OKOpen the app
  • HOLD OKOptions: move, remove, hide, uninstall
  • ▼Other apps (hold OK there to add an app to home)
  • ▲Top bar: Wi-Fi, VPN, launcher settings, TV settings
▲ ◀ OK ▶ ▼

Install in three steps

  1. 1

    Download

    Get simple-tv-launcher-vX.X.X.apk from the latest release.

  2. 2

    Copy & install

    Move it to your TV with a USB drive, LocalSend or a file manager, then open it to install.

  3. 3

    Make it home

    Open Simple TV Launcher and set it as your default launcher so the Home button always lands here.

Default launcher

One press of Home.
You're back.

Set Simple TV Launcher as your default launcher and it takes over the Home button on your remote. Switch it on in Settings → Default Launcher.

  • 🏠 Home from anywhere. Press Home in any app and you land straight on your carousel.
  • ⏻ Ready at power-on. It opens by itself when the TV starts.
  • ↩ Easy to undo. Hand the Home button back to your old launcher any time.
